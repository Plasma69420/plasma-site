1Who we are

Strive Cheats is an online store for game software and HWID spoofers, covering Fortnite, Rainbow Six Siege, Rust, Arc Raiders and Roblox. We are a small team of players and developers, and we build and update our products ourselves.

2How orders work

You choose a product and plan on strivecheats.store and pay at a secure checkout hosted by our payment provider. Your license key is delivered right after payment, and you redeem it with our Discord bot.

All products are digital. Please read our Refund Policy and Terms of Service before buying.

3Support

Support is handled in our Discord server. Open a ticket and our staff will help with setup, keys and orders. We also run a Telegram group. Step by step guides are available to customers.

4Where our reviews come from

The rating on our homepage comes from vouches left by customers in our Discord server and on this site. Because they are in our own server, they are not independent. You can also read and leave reviews on Trustpilot, and you can check what customers say on the reviews page.

5Our status page

Every product has a live status on the status page, so you can see if it is undetected, updating or down before you buy. We show when each product was last updated.

6Be careful

Use of cheats may break a game’s rules and can lead to account or hardware bans. We cannot guarantee any product will never be detected. Only buy from our official site and Discord, and never share your license key.
