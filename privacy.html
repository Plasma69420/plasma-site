Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Plasma ("Plasma," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit plasmatools.org, make a purchase, or contact support. By using our website or services, you agree to this policy. Related rules are in our Terms of Service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1. Account and order details you provide, including email address, cart contents, product selections, and support messages. If you log in with Discord, we receive your Discord ID, username and avatar.

1.2. Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited.

1.3. Payment processors collect billing information on their own checkout pages. We do not store full card numbers, CVV codes, or cryptocurrency private keys.

2. How We Use Information

2.1. To process orders, deliver product keys, send purchase emails, and provide customer support.

2.2. To prevent fraud, chargebacks, abuse, and unauthorized access to licenses.

2.3. To improve the site and understand how people find Plasma.

3. Payments

3.1. Orders are processed through our store provider, SellApp, and the payment methods it offers (PayPal, Cash App and Solana). Those companies handle payment details under their own privacy policies.

3.2. We receive payment status, order identifiers, and the amount paid so we can fulfill or decline an order. We do not sell payment data.

4. Sharing

4.1. We share information only with services needed to run the store: payment processors, email delivery, hosting, and license/key suppliers.

4.2. If you contact us on Discord, that conversation is also subject to Discord's policies.

4.3. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect Plasma, our customers, or others from fraud or abuse.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

5.1. We use browser storage to keep the site working, such as remembering your cart and your Discord login.

5.2. You can block cookies and site storage in your browser, but checkout and login features may not work correctly.

6. Data Retention & Security

6.1. Order records (email, products, delivery status) are kept as needed for support, fraud prevention, and license history.

6.2. We use HTTPS and restrict server access to order systems. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

7. Your Choices

7.1. You can contact support to ask what order email we have on file or to request deletion of support messages, subject to records we must keep for fraud, licenses, or legal reasons.

7.2. Digital goods already delivered cannot be "un-downloaded." Deleting an email record does not refund a purchase.

8. Children

Plasma is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child provided information, contact us through Discord and we will delete it where required.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through a ticket in our Discord at discord.gg/plasmatools.
