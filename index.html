Ask in Discord
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept PayPal, Cash App and Solana at checkout. If you need another option, open a ticket in our Discord and we will sort it out.
How quickly will I receive my product?
Instantly. Your key and download are delivered as soon as your payment is confirmed.
Can I get a refund?
Digital products are covered by our refund policy. If something is not working, open a ticket first. Most issues are fixed fast.
Are the cheats undetected?
We test every build before release and show live status for each product on the status page. No software is risk-free, so check the status before you play.
What about the full access accounts?
Fortnite Accounts are full access accounts delivered after payment. Stock and what is included are listed on the accounts page.
How can I get help with my order?
Open a ticket in our Discord with your order ID and the team will help you.
What are HWID spoofers?
A spoofer changes the hardware identifiers that games use to recognise your PC. A temporary spoofer lasts until you restart, a permanent spoofer changes them for good.
